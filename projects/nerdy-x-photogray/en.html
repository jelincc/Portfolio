Purpose · Background

A New Kind of Collab, Built Fast on a Minimal Structure

For our collab with in-house apparel brand 'Nerdy,' we added a brand-new AI filter. Since the existing photo booth flow couldn't be reused as-is, we needed to plan and design a new event screen from scratch. We worked with the PHOTOGRAY development team to keep even the server architecture as lean as possible, so we could launch quickly.

Design

From Theme Selection to Shooting: A 3-Theme Rotation

We built it so images from three different themes rotate every 3 seconds. After reading the instructions, users pick a gender and a music track, then move straight into shooting photos and video.

QR Download Page

This is where users can download their theme photos and video within the time limit.

Decisions & Trade-offs

The Video Download Delay, and Two Countermeasures

Problem: Since the AI video wasn't downloadable until 12 hours later, both social mentions and download counts came in low.
01Surface the wait time on the first download screen
ResponseWe removed the download-availability time that used to sit at the top of the original design, and instead highlighted the wait time directly on the button so users could notice it much more easily.

Revised Download Page

02Emphasize that video is available from the photo booth
ResponseThe original printing screen only gave a basic prompt like "Check your photo and video." We improved this by placing the actual moving video on the left to catch the eye, and made the copy itself stand out more.
