Purpose · Hypothesis

Manual operations that made even one collab take days to launch

Every time we ran a photo booth brand collab, steps like frame registration, store deployment, and settlement were all handled manually. With so many steps and so much riding on individual staff, getting a single collab ready took a long time.

Audience

Internal operations team, collab partner brands

Problem

Collab prep, deployment, and settlement all done manually, in an operation that depended heavily on people

Goal

Automate operational steps to cut lead time and reduce dependency on manual labor

If:

We consolidate the scattered collab-operations process into one admin

Then:

Reliance on individual staff will drop, and the lead time to deploy nationwide will shrink significantly

Deliverables

Collab management and campaign operations in one admin

Built the tab structure around the project's actual stages (Requested → Design in Progress → Testing in Progress → Deployment History), and designed the UX and visuals for each stage.

Collab Management

Designed a management screen that handles everything step by step, from registering a brand collab through frame deployment and per-store display settings. Data is applied to the flagship booth first for testing and iteration, then rolled out to booths nationwide.

Operations Automation Flow

Standardized the workflow into 12 steps minimum, restructured so a nationwide rollout can be handled entirely inside the admin. Per-frame data is aggregated at deployment, and edits can be made and redeployed whenever needed.

Design System

Defined admin-specific components (tables, filters, status badges, and more) from scratch, built to be reusable across other admin screens going forward.

Collab Booth Management · Status

Defined four status badges (Frame in Progress, Booth in Progress, Images Complete, Registration Complete) so anyone can tell which stage a collab is in just by scanning the list.

Editing a Collab · Real Data Example

Even after registration, owners can edit frame and booth data directly, check the live preview, and redeploy right away.

Deployment History

Each collab's live period and performance (count, daily average) are tallied cumulatively from the moment it's deployed, all visible on one screen.
